Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-19

Fat Stack is a Telegram Mini App. We try to collect as little data as possible.

What we store on your device

  • Your score, best score, streak, settings, and purchase grants — all in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What we receive from Telegram

  • Your Telegram user ID, first name, and language code (via the standard Telegram Mini App initData handshake) — only when you launch the game from Telegram.
  • We use this to authenticate Telegram Stars purchases and (if you opt in) to send you in-app bot notifications (e.g. daily challenge reminders).

Telegram Stars payments

  • Purchases are processed entirely by Telegram. We never see your card or wallet details.
  • Telegram tells us only: which SKU you bought, when, and your Telegram user ID, so we can credit the in-game item.

What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising. No ad SDKs. No tracking pixels.
  • No selling, sharing, or renting any data to anyone.
  • No analytics on individual people — only anonymous, aggregated session counts on the server.

Notifications

If you've messaged our bot (e.g. tapped /start), we may send you up to 3 notifications per day — streak reminders, daily challenges, comeback bonuses. You can mute or block the bot in Telegram at any time.

Children

Fat Stack is not directed to children under 13. Telegram itself requires users to be 13+.

Contact

Questions? Reach us on Telegram via the bot, or reply to any of its messages.
